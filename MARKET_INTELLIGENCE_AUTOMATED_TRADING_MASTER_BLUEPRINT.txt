MARKET INTELLIGENCE & AUTOMATED TRADING PLATFORM

Master Idea, Workflow & Blueprint

1. PRODUCT VISION

Build an India-focused Market Intelligence + Quant Research + Automated Trading Platform using broker/exchange data, quantitative analytics, market structure, derivatives, order-flow intelligence, statistical research, machine learning, and controlled automated execution.

Core workflow:

DATA → MARKET STATE → MARKET INTELLIGENCE → REGIME → SCANNER → TRADE TYPE → STRATEGY → OPPORTUNITY → PROBABILITY → EXPECTED VALUE → RISK → OMS → EXECUTION → POSITION → JOURNAL → PATTERN DNA → RESEARCH / ML → IMPROVED INTELLIGENCE

3. MARKET DATA

Collect LTP, OHLC, volume, turnover, bid/ask, spread, depth, trades, timestamps and market status.

Futures: underlying, expiry, LTP, volume, OI, OI change, basis, premium/discount, VWAP, volatility, rollover.

Options: underlying, spot/future, strike, expiry, CE/PE, LTP, volume, OI, OI change, IV, Greeks, bid/ask.

Broker: orders, status, fills, positions, margin and reconciliation data.

4. DATA NORMALIZATION & QUALITY

Convert broker-specific events into canonical QuoteEvent, TradeEvent, DepthEvent, CandleEvent, MarketStatusEvent, FutureSnapshot, OptionSnapshot, OrderEvent, FillEvent and PositionEvent.

Validate timestamps, instruments, duplicates, ordering, gaps, staleness and market sessions.

Data states: HEALTHY, DEGRADED, STALE, INVALID.

5. STORAGE

Redis: hot state, latest prices/quotes/depth, current candles, regime, active signals.

ClickHouse: ticks, trades, quotes, depth, candles, futures, options, features, structure, liquidity, profiles, regimes and scanner results.

PostgreSQL: users, strategies, versions, Trade Types, risk profiles, orders, fills, positions, watchlists, backtests, models, permissions and audit.

Parquet + S3/MinIO: raw, historical, research, ML, replay and order-book datasets.

6. CANDLE ENGINE

Convert ticks/events into deterministic 1s, 1m, 3m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h and daily candles.

Handle duplicates, out-of-order events, gaps, session boundaries, partial/closed candles, volume and VWAP.

Closed candles are immutable.

7. FEATURE ENGINE

Price: returns, ATR, volatility, gaps, momentum, VWAP and moving averages.

Volume: RVOL, anomalies, acceleration, turnover, volume profile.

Structure: swing highs/lows, HH, HL, LH, LL, BOS, CHoCH, MSS, displacement, compression and expansion.

Liquidity: equal highs/lows, PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL, session liquidity, range liquidity and sweeps.

8. SMC / ICT / WYCKOFF

Treat these as measurable hypotheses, not guaranteed predictive truths.

SMC/ICT: liquidity sweep, BOS, CHoCH, MSS, FVG, displacement, order block, premium/discount.

Wyckoff: Spring, Upthrust, SOS, SOW, climax, absorption and test.

Formal sequence:

Liquidity Pool → Sweep → Reaction → Displacement → Structure Change → Retest.

9. MARKET / AUCTION PROFILE

POC, VAH, VAL, HVN, LVN, initial balance, opening range, value migration, acceptance, rejection, failed auction and excess.

10. ORDER FLOW / MICROSTRUCTURE

OFI, CVD, bid/ask imbalance, microprice, queue imbalance, depth imbalance, absorption, exhaustion, replenishment, cancellation, liquidity vacuum, price impact, Kyle lambda, Amihud illiquidity, change points, entropy and Hurst where data supports them.

11. FUTURES INTELLIGENCE

Spot → Near Future → Next Future → Far Future.

Calculate price change, volume, OI, OI change, basis, premium/discount, rollover, contango/backwardation, long buildup, short buildup, short covering and long unwinding.

12. OPTIONS INTELLIGENCE

Full option chain with OI, volume, IV, Greeks and bid/ask.

Analytics: ATM IV, IV Rank, IV Percentile, expected move, skew, term structure, call/put concentration, unusual activity and model-derived gamma exposure estimates.

13. BREADTH, SECTORS & GLOBAL

Breadth: advance/decline, up/down volume, new highs/lows, % above 20/50/200 DMA, F&O breadth, momentum breadth and divergences.

Sectors: strength, breadth, momentum, relative strength, volume, dispersion, futures positioning, options activity and rotation.

Global: US, Europe, Asia, bonds/rates, dollar, commodities and FX.

14. MARKET REGIME

TREND_UP, TREND_DOWN, RANGE, BREAKOUT, COMPRESSION, VOLATILITY_EXPANSION, VOLATILITY_CONTRACTION, PANIC, RECOVERY, MOMENTUM, MEAN_REVERSION, TRANSITION, HIGH_LIQUIDITY and LOW_LIQUIDITY.

Start with transparent rules; later use HMM/change-point/ML.

15. MARKET INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD

Show market regime, confidence, breadth, sector leadership, futures positioning, options activity, unusual activity, heatmaps, global context and qualified opportunities.

The dashboard answers:

• What is happening?

• Where is money/volume/OI moving?

• Which sectors are strong/weak?

• Which instruments have unusual activity?

• What is the current regime?

16. MARKET SCANNER

Stage 1: price, volume, RVOL, ATR, liquidity, spread.

Stage 2: BOS, CHoCH, MSS, FVG, sweep, VWAP, profile.

Stage 3: OFI, CVD, depth, absorption.

Stage 4: OI, IV, skew, basis, options activity.

Stage 5: probability, EV, historical similarity, regime compatibility, execution quality and risk.

Scanner DSL supports AND/OR/NOT, nested conditions, thresholds, timeframes and cross-asset conditions.

Example:

Daily Trend = Bullish

AND 1H BOS = True

AND 15M Liquidity Sweep = True

AND 5M Displacement = True

AND RVOL > 1.5

AND Price > VWAP

AND Sector Strength > 70

AND Futures OI Increasing

17. TRADE TYPE

Trade Type is a selection/filter before automated trading, not manual trading.

SCALPING, INTRADAY, BREAKOUT, MOMENTUM, TREND FOLLOWING, MEAN REVERSION, REVERSAL, RANGE, SWING, POSITIONAL, BTST, OPTION BUYING, OPTION SELLING, FUTURES, CUSTOM.

Trade Type defines timeframe, holding period, allowed setups, execution style, stop logic, target logic, risk limits, session and liquidity requirements.

Trade Type = WHAT

Strategy = HOW

Regime = WHEN

Risk = HOW MUCH

Execution = HOW

18. STRATEGY ENGINE

Every strategy is deterministic and versioned.

Strategy consumes market state/features and produces a structured signal.

Strategies must never directly access the broker.

19. OPPORTUNITY ENGINE

Opportunity contains instrument, direction, Trade Type, strategy, regime, entry, stop, targets, structure/liquidity/volume/flow/derivatives/historical/execution scores, probability, expected R, risk and explanation.

Starting scoring weights:

structure 0.18, liquidity 0.14, volume 0.10, flow 0.14, derivatives 0.10, regime 0.12, historical 0.10, execution 0.07, risk 0.05.

Weights must be validated statistically.

20. PROBABILITY + EXPECTED VALUE

Calculate P(TP1), P(TP2), P(SL), expected R, expected duration and expected value.

Include brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST, stamp duty, spread, slippage, market impact and latency.

Probability models must be calibrated, time-aware and out-of-sample validated.

21. RISK ENGINE

Mandatory hard gate between opportunity and OMS.

Checks: strategy validity, Trade Type enabled, market open, fresh data, valid instrument, duplicate signal, existing position, daily loss, strategy loss, symbol/sector/portfolio exposure, margin, leverage, slippage and kill switch.

Any critical failure = REJECT.

22. POSITION SIZING

Risk Amount = Account Equity × Risk Fraction.

Risk Per Unit = |Entry − Stop| × Point Value.

Quantity = Risk Amount / Risk Per Unit.

Production sizing also considers lot size, margin, liquidity, volatility, spread, portfolio/sector exposure, leverage and capacity.

23. OMS

Lifecycle:

SCANNED → CANDIDATE → QUALIFIED → RISK_CHECK → ORDER_PENDING → PARTIALLY_FILLED → FILLED → MANAGED → SCALE_OUT → EXIT → CLOSED.

OMS is the application source of truth; broker state is reconciled against OMS.

24. IDEMPOTENCY

Use a key based on strategy version + instrument + signal timestamp + direction.

If the same signal is processed twice, return the existing intent/order instead of creating a duplicate.

25. PAPER TRADING

Default mode is PAPER.

Real market data → real features → intelligence → scanner → strategy → probability → risk → OMS → paper execution simulator → position → P&L.

Simulate spread, slippage, latency, partial fills, rejects, gaps, fees and liquidity.

26. LIVE TRADING SAFETY

Live execution disabled by default.

Required sequence:

Explicit approval → Production → LIVE mode → Risk Engine → OMS → Broker.

Never allow Strategy → Broker or AI → Broker.

27. POSITION MANAGER

Manage average entry, quantity, realized/unrealized P&L, stops, targets, scale-outs, exits, MFE, MAE, duration and slippage.

28. JOURNAL & PATTERN DNA

Every trade becomes research data.

Capture entry state, market state, features, signal, risk decision, execution, exit, MFE, MAE, R, duration, slippage, regime and strategy version.

Pattern DNA:

Trend, volatility, volume, RVOL, structure, liquidity, OFI, CVD, OI, IV, skew, breadth, sector, regime and time of day.

Workflow:

Current Setup → Similar Historical Setups → Outcome Distribution → Probability → Expected Value.

29. BACKTESTING

Historical Data → Market State → Features → Market Intelligence → Scanner → Trade Type → Strategy → Risk Engine → Order Simulation → Fill Model → Position → P&L.

Validate with in-sample, out-of-sample, walk-forward, parameter sensitivity, Monte Carlo, regime analysis, cost sensitivity and capacity analysis.

Prevent look-ahead bias, leakage, survivorship bias and unrealistic fills/costs.

30. AI / ML

ML: setup classification, outcome probability, regime classification, similarity, ranking and anomaly detection.

AI: market explanation, opportunity explanation, scanner-rule generation, research hypotheses, backtest analysis, journal analysis and pattern discovery.

AI is an intelligence/research layer and cannot bypass Quant, Risk or OMS.

31. FRONTEND

Navigation:

Dashboard, Markets, Equities, Futures, Options, Indices, Sectors, Institutional, Global, Breadth, Heatmaps, Scanner, Opportunities, Trade Types, Strategies, Paper Trading, Positions, Orders, Journal, Pattern DNA, Backtesting, Research, Models, Settings.

Professional chart layers:

Price, volume, VWAP, structure, liquidity, SMC/ICT, profile, order flow, derivatives, signals, execution and research.

Realtime chart:

Upstox WebSocket → Normalizer → Tick State → Candle Aggregator → Feature Engine → Chart Publisher → Realtime Gateway → Browser Store → Chart Renderer.

32. VERSIONING

Version data, features, strategies, scanner, risk profiles, models and execution.

Every historical trade must be reproducible.

33. RESEARCH WORKFLOW

Question → Dataset → Feature Version → Hypothesis → Experiment → Backtest → Validation → Statistical Analysis → Conclusion → Strategy Version.

34. OBSERVABILITY

Track market-data latency, stale feeds, dropped events, sequence gaps, normalization errors, feature/scanner latency, opportunities, probability distributions, model drift, orders, rejects, fills, slippage, execution latency and infrastructure health.

Use structured logs, metrics, tracing, alerts and audit logs.

35. DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Phase 1 Foundation: backend, frontend, Docker, PostgreSQL, Redis, ClickHouse, configuration, logging and testing.

Phase 2 Market Data: Upstox authentication, instrument master, WebSocket, protobuf, normalization, data quality, reconnect and subscriptions.

Phase 3 Market State: candle engine, VWAP, ATR, volume, OHLC and multi-timeframe state.

Phase 4 Market Intelligence: structure, liquidity, SMC/ICT, Wyckoff, profile, order flow, futures, options, breadth, sectors, global and regime.

Phase 5 Scanner: broad market scanner, DSL, multi-timeframe conditions, cross-asset conditions, ranking and explanations.

Phase 6 Automated Trading: Trade Type, strategy, opportunity, probability, EV, risk, OMS and paper execution.

Phase 7 Research: journal, Pattern DNA, backtester, walk-forward, Monte Carlo, costs, capacity, ML and probability calibration.

Phase 8 Production: observability, reconciliation, recovery, HA, disaster recovery, security, audit and controlled live execution.

36. GOLDEN RULES

1. Data first.

2. Bad data must never produce a trusted signal.

3. Market Intelligence comes before Automated Trading.

4. Trade Type is a pre-trading filter, not manual execution.

5. Strategy does not directly access the broker.

6. Probability must be calibrated.

7. Expected Value must include costs.

8. Risk Engine is mandatory.

9. OMS controls execution.

10. Paper Trading comes before Live Trading.

11. AI cannot bypass Quant/Risk/OMS.

12. Every trade becomes research data.

13. Every strategy/model/signal must be versioned.

14. Backtest and live logic share the same conceptual pipeline.

15. No look-ahead bias.

16. No live execution by default.

FINAL PRODUCT

A quantitative Market Intelligence system that converts real-time market data into measurable market state, identifies statistically qualified opportunities, filters them by Trade Type and Strategy, validates probability and expected value, enforces risk through a centralized Risk Engine, executes through an OMS, and continuously learns from historical outcomes.
